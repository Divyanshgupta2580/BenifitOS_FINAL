# Phase 1 — Enterprise Governance Foundation

---

# 1. Purpose

The Enterprise Governance Architecture defines the organizational structures, decision-making processes, governance principles, policies, and accountability mechanisms that guide the evolution of BenefitOS.

Its objective is to ensure that every architectural, technical, operational, security, AI, and business decision aligns with the long-term vision of the platform while maintaining consistency, compliance, transparency, and operational excellence.

This document establishes the governance framework through which BenefitOS is planned, developed, operated, and continuously improved.

# 12. Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

Every significant architectural decision is documented as an Architecture Decision Record.

Each ADR contains:

- Decision Identifier
- Context
- Problem Statement
- Alternatives Considered
- Selected Decision
- Technical Justification
- Risks
- Consequences
- Approval Date
- Decision Owner

ADRs provide long-term architectural traceability.
