# 1. Introduction

BenefitOS is designed to serve a diverse population of Indian citizens seeking government welfare benefits.

Each user group has different goals, digital literacy levels, financial backgrounds, and documentation requirements.

Understanding these differences is essential for designing an inclusive, accessible, and citizen-first platform.

This document defines representative user personas that guide product decisions, user experience, recommendation logic, accessibility improvements, and AI interactions.

---

# 2. Purpose

The objectives of these personas are to:

- Understand user needs.
- Identify pain points.
- Improve accessibility.
- Guide feature prioritization.
- Improve AI personalization.
- Support UX decisions.
- Ensure citizen-first development.

These personas represent typical users rather than individual citizens.

---

# 3. User Segmentation

BenefitOS serves multiple categories of citizens.

Primary user groups include:

- Students
- Farmers
- Women
- Senior Citizens
- Job Seekers
- Entrepreneurs
- Persons with Disabilities
- General Citizens

Each group has unique welfare requirements and interaction patterns.

# 6. Persona Journey Summary

Every citizen follows a similar high-level journey.

```text
Register

↓

Complete Digital Twin

↓

Upload Documents

↓

Receive Recommendations

↓

Understand Eligibility

↓

Prepare Missing Documents

↓

Track Readiness

↓

Apply on Official Portal

↓

Monitor Progress
```

The exact journey varies based on citizen profile and available schemes.

# 7. Accessibility Considerations

BenefitOS shall support users with varying digital abilities.

The platform should provide:

- Clear navigation.
- High contrast.
- Keyboard accessibility.
- Screen reader support.
- Simple language.
- Responsive layouts.
- Large touch targets.
- Error prevention.
- Meaningful feedback.

Accessibility is considered a core product requirement.

---

# 8. Design Implications

The personas influence several design decisions.

Examples include:

- Minimal interface complexity.
- Professional government-style UI.
- Progressive disclosure of information.
- Step-by-step workflows.
- Consistent navigation.
- Mobile-friendly layouts.
- Clear status indicators.

The design should prioritize clarity over visual complexity.

---

# 9. AI Personalization

The AI Copilot shall adapt responses based on the citizen's verified profile.

Examples include:

- Student-focused scholarship guidance.
- Farmer-focused agricultural support.
- Entrepreneur-focused business assistance.
- Senior citizen-friendly explanations.
- Disability-aware recommendations.

Personalization shall rely only on verified profile information and user interactions.

AI shall not infer or fabricate personal attributes beyond available data.

---

# 10. Persona Summary

The BenefitOS personas represent the primary citizens the platform is designed to serve.

These personas ensure that every product decision, interface, recommendation, and AI interaction remains focused on solving real citizen problems while maintaining accessibility, transparency, and trust.

As the platform evolves, additional personas may be introduced to support new stakeholder groups and expanded platform capabilities.
